About Us

An international shipping and freight forwarding company from Pakistan — sea, air and land freight, packing, relocation, warehousing and custom brokerage, led by industry experts with 2 decades of experience.

Who we are

One Window Operation

Hi Sea Shipping is an international Shipping and Freight Forwarding company that offers a complete range shipping services like sea freight, air freight and land freight, Packing, Relocation, Warehousing and Custom brokerage by providing the highest levels of customer satisfaction to our customers.

Hi Sea Shipping is led by industry experts who are having rich experience in the field of shipping and logistics for 2 decades and working towards excellence, and we pride ourselves as leading service provider to meet challenges and perfection at work. By keeping relation with multiple carriers, we can provide the solution to any shipping requirement and serve as one window operation for all products moving through your supply chain. We have access to very competitive rates, which are fair and are constantly revised to reflect industry conditions.

Hi Sea Shipping Pakistan is established in Feb, 2006 and provides a wide range of sea freight forwarding services including our core business of Packing, NVOCC operator, cargo transshipment, consolidation services for less than container load (LCL) & full container load (FCL) cargo from and to most major ports all over the world.
